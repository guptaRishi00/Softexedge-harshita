## 7. Words we own

- **"Both halves"** — the shorthand for the position
- **"No handoff"** — the mechanism
- **"Build and grow"** — the service grouping

Checked against category clichés: none of these appear in the banned list, and none
are standard agency vocabulary. "Full-service" is the category's word for this idea
and is banned.
