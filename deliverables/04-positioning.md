# Positioning — SoftexEdge

Date: 2026-08-05 · **Status: PROVISIONAL** — built on code evidence, not a client
interview. Competitor and audience research compressed and inferred (see §8).

---

## 1. The position

**SoftexEdge builds the software and runs the marketing that fills it — one team,
both halves.**

## 2. The statement

> For **founders and operators who need a product built *and* customers arriving**
> who **currently have to hire an agency to build it and a second agency to market it,
> then referee between them**,
> **SoftexEdge** is the **team**
> that **does both halves under one roof**.
> Unlike **specialist shops that hand over a build and disappear, or marketing
> agencies with nothing to market**,
> we **own the outcome end to end** — because **the people who built the thing are the
> people running the launch, so there is nobody to point at when it underperforms.**

## 3. The trade-off

**We are not the deepest specialist in any single discipline.**

A pure-play brand consultancy will out-strategise us on brand alone. A dedicated
performance agency will out-optimise us on ads alone. We are the right choice when
the handoff between those two is where your projects actually die — and the wrong
choice when you only need one of them, done at the highest possible level.

This trade-off is real, it is stateable on the site, and it is what makes the
position credible rather than a boast.

## 5. Swap Test

Substituting the competitors most likely relevant:

| Substituted | Still true? |
|---|---|
| A regional web-dev shop | ❌ No — they don't run marketing |
| A digital marketing agency | ❌ No — they don't build software |
| A large full-service firm | ⚠️ Partially — but "one team, no handoff" fails at their scale; they have separate departments and account managers |

**Passes.** The line that does the work is *one team, both halves* — not *full
service*, which every large agency claims and which is banned as a category cliché.

## 6. What this rules out

- Any claim to be the best at brand strategy, or design, or performance marketing,
  considered alone
- Positioning as a premium specialist boutique
- Competing on scale, headcount, or office footprint
- The word "full-service" — it is the category's word for this and it means nothing

## 8. Risks

1. **Provisional basis.** No client interview, no competitor research, no audience
   research. A single conversation could invalidate this.
2. **"One team" is unverified.** If SoftexEdge subcontracts the marketing side, the
   position collapses and must be withdrawn. `[[NEEDS: confirm]]`
3. **Cheaper-than-incumbent is the stronger position** if Traul's 75% confirms.
   Diarise a review once §4 resolves.
4. **Generalist perception.** Buyers may read "does both" as "does neither well." The
   trade-off statement in §3 is the mitigation and must appear on the site.

## 9. Implications

**Architecture:** the homepage must demonstrate the combination structurally, not
assert it. Services should be grouped visibly as *Build* and *Grow* rather than
listed flat as eight equal tiles — the grouping is the argument.

**Voice:** plain and concrete. The position is about removing friction between two
disciplines, so any hype undermines it. Continues the direction set in the Hero and
About rewrites earlier today.

**Proof priority:** confirming Traul is the highest-value action available to this
client. It unlocks a stronger position *and* the flagship case study.
